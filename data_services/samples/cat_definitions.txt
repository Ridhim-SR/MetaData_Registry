Data classification categories
(Model Data Sharing Framework, MeitY draft, 31 Aug 2026, section 5.1)

CAT-1  Open Access
  Definition: Fully anonymised, aggregated, or non-personal data. No risk of re-identification.
  Examples: Aggregate statistics, counts, district-level summaries, reference data.
  Access: Open, no login required.

CAT-2  Registered Access
  Definition: De-identified data useful to researchers, start-ups, and civil society. Individual identity not exposed.
  Examples: Anonymised scheme data, de-identified health datasets, economic statistics.
  Access: Login / KYC and a purpose declaration.

CAT-3  Restricted Access
  Definition: Personal or sensitive data at individual or entity level. Shared only with DPDPA-compliant consent or a specific legal mandate.
  Examples: Vehicle / DL records, Aadhaar-linked records, individual scheme benefits.
  Access: API-based, purpose-bound, annual renewal.

CAT-4  Non-Shareable
  Definition: Classified, national-security-sensitive, or legally restricted data.
  Examples: Intelligence records, Cabinet deliberations, strategic infrastructure data.
  Access: Not shared externally; internal government network only.

Rules from the framework
- When in doubt, the higher (more restrictive) category applies.
- Direct identifiers are: name, Aadhaar, PAN, mobile, email, passport number, voter ID, driving licence number, vehicle registration, account number. A dataset holding them is personal or entity-level data (CAT-3).
- Quasi-identifiers (date of birth, full address, income, employer name) and sensitive attributes (caste, religion, health condition, disability, criminal record, financial distress, migration status) also raise the risk.
- A dataset holding sensitive fields may be CAT-3; the same dataset with those fields removed may be CAT-2; aggregated geographic and scheme-level totals may be CAT-1.
